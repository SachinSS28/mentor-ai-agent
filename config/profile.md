## In one line
Civil and environmental engineer, researcher and academic: PhD (The Hong Kong Polytechnic
University, 2019) in integrated water management (water utility hydraulics, urban water
systems, wastewater process modelling, river and hydraulic infrastructure), with over a decade
of practice in Hong Kong, India, Bhutan, Nepal and Namibia.

## Specializations
- Water distribution networks: design and cost optimization with meta-heuristic and
  benchmarking algorithms, hydraulic modelling (EPANET), smart utility analysis, risk
- Wastewater treatment: plant-wide modelling, activated sludge and secondary settlers, energy
  balance, greenhouse-gas (N2O, CH4) mitigation (Shatin WWTP, Hong Kong); compact and modular
  treatment (WetSep) for rural and disaster-prone areas
- Groundwater and riverbank filtration: pathogen removal, safe pumping-well distance, aquifer
  management, microplastics and pathogen transport, climate impacts
- Rivers: morphology, sediment transport, flood management, river training (spur dykes, vanes),
  inland navigation
- Hydropower and hydraulic structures: spillways, energy dissipation, desilting chambers,
  physical (1:70 scale) and HEC-RAS modelling
- AI and machine learning: AI-enabled engineering analytics, ML combined with hydraulic models;
  AI in supply chains and logistics
- Climate-resilient and sustainable infrastructure; durability of coastal concrete

## Current research
